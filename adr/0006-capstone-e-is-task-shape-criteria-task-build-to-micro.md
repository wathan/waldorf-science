# (E) is *capstone task-shape criteria*, not a specific artifact; the task-build moves to micro, guarded by the criteria

## Context

The meso-design plan scheduled Session 2 (Jul 1) to **decide C + E together** — phase
structure *and* a specific capstone artifact — bundled because the capstone occupies the
final phase. Kru Fang's Session-2 input draft carried a specific artifact: students sort
the block's phenomena into *directly-observed / inferred / unknowable* and defend the
placements.

Two problems surfaced going into the session:

1. **Ordering bug.** Designing a capstone task well needs the competencies
   *operationalised* first — what authentic grade-12 evidence looks like. But the plan
   schedules **(G) operationalisations** for the school break → Session 4, and Session 1
   already **deferred *which* sub-abilities to micro**. So E-the-specific-task sat *before*
   its own prerequisite.
2. **The specific draft artifact was low-confidence** — worked while tired, not fully
   engaged — so anchoring the group on it (even as a worked example) risked laundering
   unvetted work into a decision.

## Decision

**(E) is redefined** from "a specific capstone artifact" to **block-specific capstone
task-shape criteria** — what a grade-12 capstone task for *this* block must and must not
be. The **task-build itself moves to micro**, a bet the pilot teachers place and the pilot
tests, guarded by the ratified criteria.

1. **Two-tier criteria.**
   - **Tier-1 — hard constraints, ratified now.** (a) the task forces a *synthetic
     boundary-drawing* move, not bin-application (grade-12 pitch, cf.
     [ADR-0004](0004-ethical-judgement-gap-demoted.md)); (b) **class-biography safety** —
     de-personalised contest, no live personal defense (standing constraint); (c)
     **C→E coherence** — the graded phenomena must be ones the earlier phases actually
     built. Constraint (c) is *stated* now and *verified* at the Session-3 coherence check,
     which **owns the C→E seam**.
   - **Tier-2 — open, the pilot answers.** Task format, difficulty pitch, student-facing
     register, straddler count, contest mechanics. Deliberately not fixed: **task-design
     fit — whether a task actually meets these students — is an explicit pilot learning
     target**, not knowable at meso.

2. **C is decided today, unbundled from E-the-task.** The phase arc (seeing → pivot →
   inferring → boundary) needs only that *a* capstone occupies Phase 4, not the specific
   task. C ratifies on "Phase 4 = capstone-shaped, task deferred to micro."

3. **Origination today = prompt-only brainstorm.** Kru Fang brings a blank two-tier
   scaffold and states Tier-1 (b)/(c) as the *frame* the brainstorm operates within — not
   a pre-filled criteria list to edit. The group generates the criteria live. This keeps
   architect-Kru-Fang's voice off the criteria (same guard as
   [ADR-0005](0005-fork-criterion-ranked-before-read.md)). Safety and coherence are givens,
   not brainstorm fodder, so a well-meaning "let them defend their own line" cannot surface
   and have to be shot down in-room.

4. **The sort-artifact draft is fully archived** — superseded private working paper, not
   carried into the session as worked example or test case.

## Considered alternatives

- **Decide the specific task today as planned** (rejected): violates the ordering bug —
  designing the task before criteria/operationalisation — and would ratify low-confidence
  draft work.
- **Redefine E but keep the sort as a worked example / live test case** (hybrid, initially
  chosen then retracted): the draft was too low-confidence to seed the room; even as a test
  case it anchors the brainstorm.
- **Make the criteria grade-12-general (a framework-level companion, not one of the seven)**
  (rejected): doing grade-level work as a block move is the category error ADR-0003 warns
  against; and the criteria are pilot-specific (epistemic sort, contested phenomena,
  this class's biography). Genuinely general heuristics can be *lifted up* later.
- **Add a session to decide E-the-task at the working-group table** (rejected): unnecessary;
  the pilot teachers are the micro designers and the criteria + Session-3 coherence check
  are sufficient guard.

## Consequences

- **Seven outputs stay seven.** (E) keeps its slot, redefined. Its old definition already
  deferred the "specific deliverable" to micro — this lowers meso's specificity from "a
  chosen artifact concept" to "criteria the micro task must satisfy."
- **C and E decouple at decision time.** The plan's "decide C + E together" rationale no
  longer holds; C is decided today, E-criteria ratified today, E-task built at micro.
- **Session 3** coherence check gains explicit ownership of the C→E seam (Tier-1 (c)) and
  now checks the criteria + micro-bet fit, not a decided artifact.
- **Pilot evaluation** gains a named target: task-design fit. Feeds the deferred
  pilot-evaluation-criteria concern.
- **Edits**: `CONTEXT.md` (redefine (E); add *capstone task-shape criteria* and
  *task-design fit as pilot learning target*), `source/meso-design-plan-…` (Session-2
  row/protocol, seven-outputs (E), origination table, Session-3 seam, deferred section),
  `source/session2-phase-structure-draft.md` (rewrite to prompt-only brief; sort archived).
- **Published HTML + TH** regeneration deferred to the bilingual publish step.
