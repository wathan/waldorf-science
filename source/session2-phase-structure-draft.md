## What C is anchored to (decided Session 1)

- **(B) Deep question** — *"How do we know what's out there — what can light tell us, and where does seeing end and inferring begin?"*
- **(A) Competencies** — S1, S2, S3 (science strand), epistemic centre of gravity. Sub-abilities deferred to micro.
- **Connecting image** — Plato's allegory of the cave (light, shadow, what is and is not directly knowable).

The phase arc is built to *enact* the deep question: it walks the class from **seeing** (direct perception) through the **pivot** (light becomes evidence) into **inferring** (cosmology from light), then **reflects on the boundary** itself (capstone). "Where seeing ends and inferring begins" is not just asked — it is the spine of the sequence.

## Integration mechanism (from handoff — unchanged)

Main-lesson scheduling rules out co-taught blocks. The block runs as **synchronised, individually-led teaching with a rotating "main" role**. The teacher who is *secondary* in a phase poses recurring **bridge questions** (output D, pilot teachers draft) linking to the other discipline. Two teachers: **optics teacher** and **cosmology teacher** (Kru Fang / Kru X map to the two disciplines).

## Phase structure — 6-week block

*Block length confirmed: **6 weeks**. Week-to-phase spans below remain provisional pending the main-lesson calendar (a micro/scheduling input). Pilot begins Aug 17.*

| Phase | Weeks | Main | Secondary | Move in the deep question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Phenomenology of light** | wk 1–2 | Optics | Cosmology | **Seeing.** Direct, phenomenological engagement with light (colour, shadow, refraction, image). Establishes *what is directly perceived*, before any instrument or inference. |
| **2 — Spectroscopy / redshift pivot** | wk 3 | Co-lead | — | **The hinge.** Light becomes *evidence*: spectroscopy reads composition, redshift reads motion. The class crosses from seeing-the-thing to reading-light-about-a-thing-unseen. |
| **3 — Cosmology by inference** | wk 4–5 | Cosmology | Optics | **Inferring.** What we claim about "what's out there" is built from light: distance ladder, redshift, expansion. Foregrounds the chain of inference and the **expert mediation / consensus** the claims depend on. |
| **4 — Epistemological capstone** | wk 6 (final days) | Co-lead | — | **The boundary, made explicit.** Plato's cave. Students reflect on the whole arc and produce the capstone task (built at micro; E ratifies its *shape*, not the task). |

### Competency centre of gravity per phase *(emphasis, not a sub-ability lock — micro decides which)*

- **Phase 1** — **S1** (explain phenomena; hold the directly-perceived picture of light).
- **Phase 2** — **S2** (interpret data across representations; what spectra/redshift can and cannot establish).
- **Phase 3** — **S2 + S3** (inference from data; the **S3 deepen** — argument acknowledging the expert mediation and consensus "what's out there" rests on).
- **Phase 4** — **S1 epistemic emphasis** (what scientific explanation can and cannot establish; the knower-stance) drawing the whole arc together.

## Capstone task-shape criteria (E) — prompt-only brainstorm brief for Session 2

**What today ratifies is *criteria*, not a task.** Output (E) is redefined (see [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-capstone-e-is-task-shape-criteria-task-build-to-micro.md)): the group decides what a grade-12 capstone task *for this block* must and must not be. The **specific task is built at micro** — a bet the pilot teachers place, guarded by these criteria. **Task-design fit** — whether a task actually meets these students — is a Tier-2 open question and an explicit **pilot learning target**, not decided today.

**Why the shift.** Designing the task well needs the competencies operationalised first (what authentic grade-12 evidence looks like), but operationalisations (G) and sub-ability choice sit later / at micro — so a specific task would sit ahead of its own prerequisite. Criteria we *can* fix now; the task follows.

### How Session 2 runs E — prompt-only brainstorm

Kru Fang brings **a blank two-tier scaffold, not a criteria list.** The group generates the criteria live. Two Tier-1 items are stated as the **frame the brainstorm works within** — givens, not brainstorm fodder:

- **Class-biography safety** — de-personalised contest, no live personal defense. A standing constraint; re-opening it risks a well-meaning "let them defend their own line" surfacing and having to be shot down in-room.
- **C→E coherence** — graded phenomena must be ones the earlier phases actually built (the C→E seam), *verified* at the Session-3 coherence check.

Everything else — the should/shouldn't of a grade-12 capstone task — is brainstormed cold. Prompt-only keeps architect-Kru-Fang's voice off the criteria themselves (same guard as [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-fork-criterion-ranked-before-read.md)).

### The two-tier shape to fill

- **Tier-1 — hard constraints (ratify today).** Any micro task must satisfy these. Seed set (the brainstorm sharpens/extends):
  1. **Synthetic, not categorical.** The task forces a *boundary-drawing* move the student must locate and defend — not bin-application against a given taxonomy (that is grade-11 analytical pitch; the developmental-pitch risk for Candidate 1, cf. [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-ethical-judgement-gap-demoted.md)). The categories are the *product* of the student's epistemology, not a given they consume.
  2. **Class-biography safety** — de-personalised (anonymised and/or assigned positions), no live personal defense. *(Frame — given, not brainstormed.)*
  3. **C→E coherence** — graded phenomena are ones the phase arc actually builds. *(Frame — stated today, verified Session 3.)*
- **Tier-2 — open (the pilot answers).** Deliberately not fixed: exact task format, difficulty pitch, student-facing register, how many/which phenomena, solo-vs-contest mechanics. These are where task-design fit is *learned* from running the pilot.

### Handoff to micro

The pilot teachers build the specific task during micro against ratified Tier-1, choosing freely within Tier-2. The Session-3 coherence check verifies the C→E seam before the break. The archived ["sort" draft](archive/session2-capstone-sort-superseded.md) is **not** carried in as a template — it is superseded.
