## What C is anchored to (decided Session 1)

- **(B) Deep question** — *"How do we know what's out there — what can light tell us, and where does seeing end and inferring begin?"*
- **(A) Competencies** — S1, S2, S3 (science strand), epistemic centre of gravity. Sub-abilities deferred to micro.
- **Connecting image** — Plato's allegory of the cave (light, shadow, what is and is not directly knowable).

The phase arc is built to *enact* the deep question: it walks the class from **seeing** (direct perception) through the **pivot** (light becomes evidence) into **inferring** (cosmology from light), then **reflects on the boundary** itself (capstone). "Where seeing ends and inferring begins" is not just asked — it is the spine of the sequence.

## Integration mechanism (from handoff — unchanged)

Main-lesson scheduling rules out co-taught blocks. The block runs as **synchronised, individually-led teaching with a rotating "main" role**. The teacher who is *secondary* in a phase poses recurring **bridge questions** (output D, pilot teachers draft) linking to the other discipline. Two teachers: **optics teacher** and **cosmology teacher** (Kru Fang / Kru X map to the two disciplines).

## Phase structure — ≈6-week block

*Week spans are provisional; exact block length and main-lesson calendar are a micro/scheduling input. Pilot begins Aug 17.*

| Phase | Weeks | Main | Secondary | Move in the deep question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Phenomenology of light** | wk 1–2 | Optics | Cosmology | **Seeing.** Direct, phenomenological engagement with light (colour, shadow, refraction, image). Establishes *what is directly perceived*, before any instrument or inference. |
| **2 — Spectroscopy / redshift pivot** | wk 3 | Co-lead | — | **The hinge.** Light becomes *evidence*: spectroscopy reads composition, redshift reads motion. The class crosses from seeing-the-thing to reading-light-about-a-thing-unseen. |
| **3 — Cosmology by inference** | wk 4–5 | Cosmology | Optics | **Inferring.** What we claim about "what's out there" is built from light: distance ladder, redshift, expansion. Foregrounds the chain of inference and the **expert mediation / consensus** the claims depend on. |
| **4 — Epistemological capstone** | wk 6 (final days) | Co-lead | — | **The boundary, made explicit.** Plato's cave. Students reflect on the whole arc and produce the capstone artifact (E). |

### Competency centre of gravity per phase *(emphasis, not a sub-ability lock — micro decides which)*

- **Phase 1** — **S1** (explain phenomena; hold the directly-perceived picture of light).
- **Phase 2** — **S2** (interpret data across representations; what spectra/redshift can and cannot establish).
- **Phase 3** — **S2 + S3** (inference from data; the **S3 deepen** — argument acknowledging the expert mediation and consensus "what's out there" rests on).
- **Phase 4** — **S1 epistemic emphasis** (what scientific explanation can and cannot establish; the knower-stance) drawing the whole arc together.

## Capstone artifact (E) — drafted with C, for Session 2

Carried from the handoff draft, fits Candidate 1 directly: **students sort the block's phenomena into *directly observed / inferred / unknowable*** and justify each placement. The artifact *is* the deep question's answer-in-practice — it makes "where seeing ends and inferring begins" a thing the student must draw a line through and defend. Doubles as authentic evidence for the S2 (interpret data) and S1 (knower-stance) emphases. Detail of the specific deliverable → micro.
